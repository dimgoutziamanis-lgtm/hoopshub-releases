# HoopsHub

A basketball training app that counts your shots with the camera and keeps your
whole training routine in one place.

**[⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/dimgoutziamanis-lgtm/hoopshub-releases/releases/latest)**

## Features

- **AI shot counter**: point your phone at the hoop and HoopsHub detects the
  ball and rim on-device, counts your shots, and works out which zone of the
  court each shot came from.
- **Session history and progress**: review past sessions and follow your
  progress on charts.
- **Gym workouts**: build strength programs, run them with a workout timer
  and tick off exercises as you go.
- **Technical drills**: organize skill drills by category with targets and
  notes.
- **Week planner and training calendar**: schedule sessions, repeat them
  weekly, and keep a training log.
- **Diet**: plan meals with calories and protein.
- **Excel import**: load workout programs and meal plans from `.xlsx`
  spreadsheets.

## Install on Android

1. On your phone, open the
   [latest release](https://github.com/dimgoutziamanis-lgtm/hoopshub-releases/releases/latest)
   and download **HoopsHub.apk**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file
   manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**. Updates install the same way and keep your data.

## Install on iPhone

HoopsHub isn't on the App Store, so iPhone installs use the free tool
SideStore. You need a Windows PC once for setup, and the app has to be
refreshed in SideStore at least once a week.

Follow the step-by-step guide: **[Installing on iPhone with SideStore](docs/ios-install-sidestore.md)**

## Notes

- HoopsHub is a personal project, provided as-is.
- All data stays on your phone. The camera is only used for the shot counter,
  and detection runs on the device.
- This repository only hosts downloads. Found a problem? Open an issue.
